# HanContext Brand Kit — Draft v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-08  
**Status:** Draft

## Brand core

**Name:** HanContext  
**Primary tagline:** Chinese, localized with context.  
**Chinese tagline:** 懂情境的中文地區化

**Working positioning:** A controllable, explainable, context-aware Chinese regional localization tool designed for consistent regional wording across the Chinese Mainland, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.

## Brand personality

Clear, calm, precise, trustworthy, modern, non-technical, and respectful of regional language differences.

Avoid sounding like a generic AI assistant, a developer console, or a basic Simplified/Traditional converter.

## Core message pillars

- Context-aware rather than one-size-fits-all.
- Explainable rather than opaque.
- Controllable rather than free-form rewriting.
- Reproducible rather than unpredictable.
- Personalizable without exposing technical configuration.

## Visual direction

The current visual draft combines:

- clean modern software-product UI;
- restrained editorial typography;
- subtle Chinese visual references rather than decorative clichés;
- calm spacing and a professional productivity-tool feel;
- a visual balance between language, context and control.

## Draft colour direction

The visual board explores a restrained palette built around:

- deep navy / ink blue for trust and structure;
- teal / blue-green for context and active states;
- warm coral / muted red as a selective accent;
- warm off-white / beige backgrounds;
- neutral greys for secondary UI and metadata.

Exact production colour values are **not locked yet** and should be finalized during the Alpha UI design phase.

## Typography direction

Use highly legible sans-serif typography for product UI and marketing copy, with strong Traditional Chinese rendering.

Priorities:

- excellent Traditional Chinese glyph quality;
- clear English / Chinese pairing;
- neutral, modern tone;
- strong readability at small UI sizes.

No final production font family is locked in v0.1.

## Logo direction

The current concept uses a compact HanContext wordmark and a simple app-mark direction that can work at desktop-app icon size.

Do not treat the generated brand-board artwork as a final production logo master. It is a visual exploration draft only.

## Language rules

Use **Chinese Mainland** rather than **Mainland China**.

Preferred regional naming:

- Chinese Mainland
- Hong Kong
- Taiwan

The product should describe regional variation neutrally and avoid implying that one regional form is inherently more correct than another.

## Tone of voice

Write in plain, confident language.

Prefer:

- “Why was this changed?”
- “Recommended”
- “Also valid”
- “Needs your decision”
- “Remember for this context”

Avoid exposing raw implementation concepts such as confidence scores, rule IDs, database priority numbers or internal source tables in ordinary product copy.

## Draft marketing lines

**Same rules. Same result. Every time.**

**Your terms. Your context. Your Chinese.**

**Explainable. Controllable. Truly yours.**

**Not just character conversion. The right Chinese for the right context.**
