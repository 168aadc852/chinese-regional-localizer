# HanContext Marketing Draft

**Version:** v0.1  
**Date:** 2026-10-08  
**Status:** Draft / exploratory marketing material

This folder stores early HanContext branding and marketing concepts for review. It does **not** claim that all described product features are already implemented.

## Working identity

**Product name:** HanContext  
**Tagline:** Chinese, localized with context.  
**中文：** 懂情境的中文地區化

**Working positioning:** A controllable, explainable, context-aware Chinese regional localization tool designed for consistent wording across the Chinese Mainland, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.

## Included files

- `brand-copy-v0.1.md` — draft brand and marketing copy.
- `brand-kit-v0.1.md` — draft identity, tone, palette and usage guidance.
- `hancontext-brand-kit-v0.1.png` — visual brand-board draft, preserved unchanged from the original PNG (1536×1024; approximately 1.8 MB).

## Terminology note

Use **Chinese Mainland** rather than **Mainland China** in future HanContext product and marketing copy.

These assets are drafts and may change before Alpha.
