# Hong Kong Government Open Data (DATA.GOV.HK)

Status: **approved_with_conditions**

## Intended use
Hong Kong official datasets that are actually distributed through DATA.GOV.HK and fall within its published Terms and Conditions of Use.

## Region coverage
Hong Kong

## Official source
URL: https://data.gov.hk/
Publisher: Digital Policy Office, Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, together with relevant organisations that publish datasets on the portal.

## Licence / terms review
Terms evidence: https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions
FAQ: https://data.gov.hk/en/faq

The portal terms expressly allow users to browse, download, distribute, reproduce, hyperlink to and print the Data, free of charge, for both commercial and non-commercial purposes, subject to conditions including source identification and attribution.

- Commercial use: **Yes**
- Reproduction: **Yes**
- Redistribution: **Yes**
- Modification / adaptation: **Not expressly granted in the wording reviewed**
- Attribution: **Required**; source, Government / relevant organisation ownership and DATA.GOV.HK attribution must be given as required by the terms
- Share-alike/copyleft: **No general ShareAlike clause identified**
- Database-specific obligations: Dataset-specific terms may still apply; review each dataset record before ingestion
- API/download restrictions: Use official dataset/API/download mechanisms and comply with dataset-specific and portal terms

## Packaging decision
Approved only for use cases clearly covered by the DATA.GOV.HK Terms of Use.

For this project:
- unmodified or faithfully normalised copies of qualifying portal data may be redistributed with required attribution;
- do **not** assume permission to create a copyright-dependent adapted/derivative terminology database from a dataset unless the dataset page or another authoritative permission clearly allows modification/adaptation;
- record the exact dataset page and publisher for every imported dataset.

## Important boundary
This approval does **not** automatically cover every Hong Kong Government website or every Department of Justice resource merely because it is publicly accessible. The Combined DoJ Glossaries are reviewed separately.

## Update method
Use official DATA.GOV.HK dataset pages, APIs or downloadable files. Store dataset URL, publisher, update date and terms snapshot/check date in provenance metadata.

## Evidence summary
- DATA.GOV.HK Terms and Conditions permit commercial and non-commercial browsing, downloading, distribution and reproduction with attribution.
- DATA.GOV.HK FAQ describes the portal as disseminating open data for free re-use, subject to the portal terms.
