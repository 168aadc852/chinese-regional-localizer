# Chinese Open WordNet

Status: **approved_with_conditions**

## Intended use
Chinese synsets, semantic senses, synonym grouping and word-sense disambiguation.

## Official source
Current distribution: Open Multilingual Wordnet data repository
Licence file: https://github.com/omwn/omw-data/blob/main/wns/cow/LICENSE
Original copyright: Francis Bond and Shan Wang

- Commercial use: **Yes**

- Warranty: Provided as-is; preserve disclaimer

## Packaging decision
Approved for a permissive/attribution-required semantic layer, subject to preserving the full required notice/disclaimer.

Recommended use:
- sense IDs / synsets;
- semantic grouping;
- disambiguation support;
- cross-checking candidate meanings.

Do not represent Chinese Open WordNet as a regional CN/HK/TW authority; it is a semantic resource.

## Update method
Use the current Open Multilingual Wordnet distribution and record the OMW release/commit plus the Chinese Open WordNet licence text used.
