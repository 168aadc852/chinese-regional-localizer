# DBnary

Status: `pending_review`

## Preliminary evidence

- Official project: https://kaiko.getalp.org/about-dbnary/
- DBnary extracts structured lexical data from Wiktionary and publishes multilingual Linguistic Linked Open Data.
- The project states that its extracted data is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0.

## Potential project use

- Structured Chinese lexical senses
- Translation relations
- Synonyms and lexical links
- Machine-friendly RDF/OntoLex ingestion
- Alternative to parsing Wiktionary wikitext directly
