# Data-Source / Licence Review

Start with:
1. the specific source record under `data-registry/`;
2. the matching entry in `data-registry/sources.yaml`.

Load only if needed:
- `docs/DATA_POLICY.md` for policy questions;
- `docs/LICENSE_PACKAGING.md` for packaging boundaries;
- official licence/terms evidence for the source being reviewed.

Do not read every source record.

Rules:
- never infer permission from public availability;
- unknown rights stay `pending_review` or `reference_only`;
- keep source record and `sources.yaml` consistent;
- record evidence, review date, allowed scope and excluded scope;
- do not broaden approval from one dataset/file to an entire website or project family.
